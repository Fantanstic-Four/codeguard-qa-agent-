## Review questions

Every member should be able to answer:

1. Who is the primary user and what single task does CodeGuard help that user complete?
2. Why is AI valuable in this workflow, and which decisions must not be delegated to it?
3. What evidence prevents CodeGuard from inventing a pass/fail result?
4. Which tools will exist, and what inputs, outputs, and permissions will they have?
5. When must the agent stop or ask for approval?
6. What data is used, who owns it, and how is restricted data excluded?
