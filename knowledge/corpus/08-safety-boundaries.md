# Safety and Authorization Boundaries
SAFE-001: CodeGuard may read team-approved requirements and documentation.
SAFE-002: CodeGuard may propose tests and summarize evidence.
SAFE-003: CodeGuard may not automatically merge a pull request.
SAFE-004: CodeGuard may not deploy to production.
SAFE-005: CodeGuard may not access secrets.
SAFE-006: CodeGuard may not execute arbitrary shell commands.
SAFE-007: Higher-impact actions introduced in later weeks require explicit authorization and, where appropriate, human approval.
