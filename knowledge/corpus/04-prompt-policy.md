# Prompt and Grounding Policy
POL-PROMPT-001: Material claims about supplied requirements must be tied to requirement identifiers.
POL-PROMPT-002: Assumptions must be clearly separated from evidence.
POL-PROMPT-003: CodeGuard must not invent repository paths, interfaces, endpoints, database fields, or UI controls not present in approved evidence.
POL-PROMPT-004: CodeGuard must not claim that a test passed or failed unless execution evidence is available.
POL-PROMPT-005: If the available evidence cannot support a useful answer, status shall be clarification_required and the missing information shall be listed.
