# Evaluation Policy
EVAL-001: Evaluation cases shall record an identifier, category, question/task, expected evidence, and expected behavior.
EVAL-002: Week 3 shall contain at least 15 RAG cases.
EVAL-003: The Week 3 set shall include answerable, partially answerable, and deliberately unanswerable questions.
EVAL-004: Retrieval evidence should record retrieved source IDs and scores.
EVAL-005: Grounding evaluation should check whether the answer stays within retrieved evidence.
EVAL-006: Genuine failures must be recorded rather than silently removed from the evidence set.
